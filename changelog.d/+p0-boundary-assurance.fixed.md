P0 assurance now builds and installs real artifacts in pristine consumers,
checks independent optional-extra installations, performs real loopback TLS
handshakes, injects filesystem/process failures, and challenges parser and token
boundaries with independent controls and bounded workers. Required CI execution
fails when declared extras are unavailable.

The tests pin fixes for positive finite process timeouts, scratch-descriptor
ownership and original-error preservation during rollback, best-effort directory
syncing, strict canonical Base64url bearer envelopes, non-ASCII MAC rejection,
and editable YAML's standard `!!str` construction. The general-purpose URL
Base64 codec retains its documented lenient behavior.
Independent audio-extra consumers also exposed and pin native WAV format
selection for file-like, extensionless and misleading-extension outputs in both
one-shot and streaming writers.
